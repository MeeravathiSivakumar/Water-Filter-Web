# M SQUARE WATERTECH - Premier Water Purification & Service Web Application

A state-of-the-art, high-performance web experience for **M SQUARE WATERTECH SERVICE LLP**, providing premier multi-brand RO water purifier services, repair, filter replacements, whole-house water softeners, and commercial water treatment solutions across Chennai.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Living Water Bubble Wordmark Engine (`msq-water-wordmark.js`)**
   - Canvas-based 60fps aquatic fluid physics simulation inside bold headline typography.
   - Realistic 3D spherical bubbles with buoyancy drift, sinusoidal wobble, specular light glints, and electric cyan/azure/teal color palettes.
   - Interactive mouse/touch repulsion physics and click-to-pop water splash droplets.
   - Hardware-accelerated two-pass offscreen canvas masking with `source-in` composite mode.

2. **Precision Multi-Stage Filtration Showcase**
   - Interactive 4-stage exploded filtration assembly (Sediment Filter, Carbon Filter, RO Membrane, Post Carbon Filter).
   - Pixel-perfect layout across desktop (1440px+) and mobile (390px, 546px) screens with zero horizontal overflow.
   - Connector lines docking callout cards directly to their respective filter stages.

3. **Liquid Metal Dynamic Button Styling**
   - High-fidelity liquid metal fluid sheen sweeping animation with cyan/aqua/royal blue crystal gradients.
   - Cohesive luxury visual branding with subtle border pulses and depth elevation.

4. **100% Fully Responsive Design**
   - Smooth scrolling, touch-optimized interactions, and mobile drawer navigation.
   - Tested across all major viewports (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile 390px, 546px).

5. **Deployments**
   - Deployed on **Vercel**: [https://msquare-watertech.vercel.app](https://msquare-watertech.vercel.app)
