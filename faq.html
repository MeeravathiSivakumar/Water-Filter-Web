How often should I get my RO water purifier serviced?
We recommend routine servicing every 3 to 6 months to replace the external pre-filter sediment candle and check water pressure. The RO membrane and activated carbon filters generally last 12 to 18 months, depending on the raw water TDS and daily usage in your Chennai locality.
Which water purifier brands do you service?
We provide comprehensive multi-brand servicing, filter replacement, RO membrane changes, and electrical repair for all major brands including Kent, Aquaguard, Pureit, Livpure, Havells, Blue Star, AO Smith, and all custom domestic or assembled RO+UV systems.

How do I know if my RO membrane or filters need replacement?
Common warning signs include: drastically slowed water dispensing, an unpleasant or brackish taste, foul smell, persistent water leakage from the bottom or filter housings, high TDS reading on purified water, or constant beeping alerts from modern electronic purifiers.
What is the difference between an RO purifier, a Water Softener, and an Iron Removal Filter?
An RO Purifier removes dissolved salts, heavy metals, and microorganisms to produce safe drinking water. A Water Softener treats whole-house water using ion-exchange resin to eliminate calcium and magnesium scaling on pipes, tiles, and appliances. An Iron Removal Filter (IRF) treats borewell water containing yellow stains, rusty smell, and dissolved iron.
